2. ENGLISH

This privacy policy explains how your information is handled when you use the Detective: Codename Truth ("Application") mobile app.

a) Collection and Use of Personal Data

Our application does not collect any personal data from users, such as name, email address, phone number, or location. There is no user registration or account creation required.

b) Local Data Storage (Room Database)

Your in-game progress (cleared cases, earned stars, rank points, and user language preferences) is stored entirely on your device's local memory. This data is never transmitted to external servers and cannot be accessed by us.

c) Third-Party Services

Our application does not integrate third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs that monitor user behavior or harvest data.

d) Children's Privacy (13+ Age Limit)

Our application is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.
